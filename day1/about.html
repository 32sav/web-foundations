About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for capturing short notes and ideas the moment they come to you. It keeps everything in one place so you can find, review and act on your thoughts later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note in the "New note" box.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button.
  3. Your note appears in the "Your notes" list.
  4. Come back any time to review your notes.

Features

  • Add notes in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • A running count of how many notes you have
  • Simple, distraction-free layout

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you typed
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field
